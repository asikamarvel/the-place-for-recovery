    Medication Management

    Collaborative, personalized care

    Our mission is to provide compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care tailored to everyone's unique needs. Medication management is a collaborative process that supports recovery with careful monitoring and clear education.

    Book Appointment Contact Us

    What medication management includes

    At The Place for Recovery, we offer comprehensive medication management to support your mental health journey. We tailor prescriptions to your needs and monitor progress carefully to improve mental wellness and stability.

    • Psychotropic medications for depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, and schizophrenia
    • Laboratory tests to monitor effectiveness and side effects, including medication levels and other relevant health markers
    • Clear explanation of benefits and side effects
    • Regular follow-ups and dose adjustments
    • Telehealth check-ins for convenience

    Collaboration with clients

    We work closely with you to find the right medication management plan, considering preferences, lifestyle, and potential side effects. This personalized approach ensures that you receive the best possible care, tailored specifically for you.

    Medication options we provide

    Medication is not one-size-fits-all. We select options based on your history, goals, and response, then monitor closely and adjust as needed.

    Antidepressants

    SSRIs, SNRIs, and other agents for depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD, and related conditions.

    Mood Stabilizers

    For bipolar disorder and mood dysregulation, with routine monitoring when needed.

    Antipsychotics

    Oral and long-acting injectable options for schizophrenia and severe mood disorders.

    ADHD Medications

    Stimulant and non-stimulant options for children and adults, tailored to your routine.

    Anti-Anxiety Options

    Responsible, targeted prescribing with long-term stability in mind.

    Long-Acting Injectables

    Monthly injectables for patients who prefer not to manage daily oral medication.
