How it works

A clear care process that keeps you informed

Getting started is easier than you think. Here is what a typical care journey looks like at The Place for Recovery.

Book your first appointment

Use our Tebra booking link or give us a call. We will find a time that works for you, in-person or via telehealth.

Comprehensive intake evaluation

Our providers take time to understand your history, goals, and challenges so the plan is built around your real life.

Your personalized treatment plan

Together you build a plan that fits you, whether that means therapy, medication, or both, with ongoing support as you progress.

Fast, secure telehealth when life is busy

We offer HIPAA-compliant telehealth visits for assessment, medication check-ins, and therapy so you can stay consistent with care from anywhere in Pennsylvania or Maryland.

Why choose us

Care designed around trust, clarity, and consistency

Our mission is to provide compassionate, evidence-based psychiatric care tailored to everyone's unique needs. We combine clinical depth with a practical human approach, so your treatment feels structured, supportive, and easy to follow.

Experienced

You are working with a provider who has years of direct psychiatry experience across anxiety, mood, trauma, ADHD, and substance use conditions.

Client-centered care

We listen first, explain clearly, and build plans that match your goals, schedule, and real-life responsibilities.

Confidential environment

Your privacy matters. Visits are judgment-free, HIPAA-compliant, and focused on helping you speak openly and move forward with confidence.
