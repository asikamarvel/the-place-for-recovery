Evidence-based therapy modalities

Our psychotherapy services encompass a variety of evidence-based therapeutic modalities. Therapy plans are personalized to goals, developmental stage, and clinical needs. Our therapists create a safe, supportive environment where you can explore your thoughts and feelings while building lasting change.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

What it does: CBT focuses on identifying and changing negative thought patterns and behaviors.

Goals for treatment: Equip clients with coping strategies and tools to manage anxiety, depression, and other mental health issues.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

What it does: DBT combines cognitive-behavioral techniques with mindfulness practices.

Goals for treatment: Build skills for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and interpersonal effectiveness.

Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)

What it does: ACT encourages individuals to accept their thoughts and feelings rather than fight them.

Goals for treatment: Increase psychological flexibility and promote a values-based life that supports personal goals.

Psychodynamic Therapy

What it does: Explores how past experiences influence present behavior.

Goals for treatment: Gain insight into unresolved conflicts and emotional difficulties while supporting personal growth.

Interpersonal Therapy (IPT)

Build healthier relationships and communication, especially during life transitions.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions in a confidential space with support tailored to your goals.

Family & Group Therapy

Strengthen support systems, improve communication, and rebuild trust together.

Play Therapy (Children)

Age-appropriate support that helps children express emotions and build coping skills.

Conditions supported through psychotherapy

We focus on healing, mental wellness, and recovery through tailored care.

Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

GAD involves persistent, uncontrollable worry about everyday life, often paired with physical tension.

  • Persistent worry or anxiety about multiple topics
  • Restlessness or feeling on edge
  • Difficulty concentrating or mind going blank
  • Muscle tension or sleep disruption

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

OCD includes intrusive thoughts and repetitive behaviors that can interfere with daily life.

  • Recurrent, intrusive thoughts or images
  • Compulsive behaviors such as checking or cleaning
  • Avoidance of triggers
  • Significant time spent on obsessions and compulsions

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

PTSD can develop after trauma and is marked by intrusive memories, avoidance, and heightened arousal.

  • Flashbacks or intrusive memories
  • Nightmares related to the trauma
  • Negative mood or beliefs
  • Difficulty sleeping or concentrating
