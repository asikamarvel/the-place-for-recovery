Comprehensive Guide to Mental Health Disorders

Each condition includes a brief description and common signs and symptoms.

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

Major Depressive Disorder is a common, yet serious mood disorder that negatively affects how a person feels, thinks, and handles daily activities.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Persistent sadness or low mood
  • Loss of interest or pleasure in most activities
  • Significant weight loss or gain
  • Insomnia or excessive sleeping
  • Fatigue or loss of energy
  • Feelings of worthlessness or excessive guilt
  • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
  • Recurrent thoughts of death or suicide

Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

Generalized Anxiety Disorder is characterized by excessive, uncontrollable worry about various aspects of life, such as work, health, and social interactions.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Persistent worry or anxiety about a variety of topics
  • Restlessness or feeling on edge
  • Fatigue or difficulty sleeping
  • Trouble concentrating or mind going blank
  • Irritability
  • Muscle tension
  • Physical symptoms such as headaches or stomachaches

Panic Disorder

Panic Disorder involves recurrent and unexpected panic attacks, which are sudden periods of intense fear or discomfort.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Rapid heart rate or palpitations
  • Sweating, trembling, or shaking
  • Shortness of breath or feeling of choking
  • Chest pain or discomfort
  • Nausea or abdominal distress
  • Dizziness, light-headedness, or feeling faint
  • Fear of losing control or "going crazy"
  • Fear of dying

Bipolar Disorder

Bipolar Disorder is marked by extreme mood swings that include emotional highs (mania or hypomania) and lows (depression).

Signs and symptoms:

  • Periods of unusually intense emotion or irritability
  • Increased activity or energy (mania or hypomania)
  • Decreased need for sleep
  • Racing thoughts or rapid speech
  • Impulsive or risky behaviors
  • Depressive episodes with feelings of sadness or hopelessness
  • Changes in sleep patterns and appetite

Schizophrenia

Schizophrenia is a chronic and severe mental health disorder that affects how a person thinks, feels, and behaves.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Hallucinations (seeing or hearing things that are not there)
  • Delusions (false beliefs)
  • Disorganized thinking or speech
  • Lack of motivation or emotional expression
  • Social withdrawal
  • Impaired functioning in daily life

Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)

OCD is characterized by persistent, unwanted thoughts (obsessions) and repetitive behaviors or mental acts (compulsions) that an individual feels driven to perform.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Recurrent, intrusive thoughts or images
  • Compulsive behaviors, such as excessive cleaning or checking
  • Avoidance of situations that trigger obsessions
  • Significant time spent on obsessions and compulsions
  • Distress or impairment in daily functioning

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)

PTSD can develop after exposure to a traumatic event and is characterized by intrusive thoughts, avoidance of reminders of the trauma, negative changes in mood, and heightened arousal.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Flashbacks or intrusive memories
  • Nightmares related to the trauma
  • Avoidance of reminders of the event
  • Negative mood or beliefs
  • Heightened startle response
  • Difficulty sleeping or concentrating

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

ADHD is a neurodevelopmental disorder characterized by inattention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity that can affect both children and adults.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Difficulty sustaining attention or following through on tasks
  • Impulsivity and difficulty waiting for one's turn
  • Hyperactivity, including fidgeting or talking excessively
  • Poor organizational skills
  • Forgetfulness in daily activities

Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD)

Autism Spectrum Disorders encompass a range of developmental conditions characterized by challenges in social interaction, communication, and repetitive behaviors.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Difficulty with social interactions and understanding social cues
  • Limited or absent verbal and non-verbal communication
  • Repetitive behaviors or routines
  • Intense focus on specific interests
  • Sensory sensitivities

Eating Disorders

Eating disorders are complex conditions that involve unhealthy eating behaviors and distorted body image.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Preoccupation with weight, food, and body shape
  • Severe restriction of food intake (anorexia)
  • Binge eating followed by purging (bulimia)
  • Eating large quantities of food without purging (binge-eating disorder)
  • Emotional distress related to eating habits

Sleep Disorders

Sleep disorders encompass problems related to sleeping too little, too much, or experiencing disrupted sleep.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep (insomnia)
  • Excessive daytime sleepiness (narcolepsy)
  • Loud snoring or gasping for air during sleep (sleep apnea)
  • Unusual behaviors during sleep, such as sleepwalking

Personality Disorders

Personality disorders involve enduring patterns of behavior, cognition, and inner experience that deviate from cultural norms and can impair functioning and relationships.

Signs and symptoms:

  • Difficulty maintaining relationships
  • Rigid and unhealthy thinking patterns
  • Intense emotional responses
  • Impulsivity or self-destructive behaviors
  • Distorted self-image
