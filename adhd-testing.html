ADHD Evaluation

ADHD testing and focused psychiatric support

If you are dealing with inattention, restlessness, forgetfulness, or impulsive decisions, a structured ADHD evaluation can provide clarity. We offer secure telehealth and office visits to support diagnosis and treatment planning.

Book Appointment Contact Us

How the ADHD evaluation works

Schedule and intake

We collect your history, current concerns, and medication details before the visit so we can use your time well.

Clinical interview and screening tools

During your appointment, we use structured interviews and validated measures while also reviewing mood, sleep, and anxiety symptoms.

Results and treatment plan

You receive clear findings and next steps, including therapy strategies, medication options when appropriate, and follow-up support.

What we assess during ADHD testing

We evaluate focus symptoms and related factors to target the right problem first.

Inattentive symptoms

Difficulty sustaining attention, forgetfulness, disorganization, and task completion challenges.

Hyperactive-impulsive symptoms

Restlessness, fidgeting, interrupting, or feeling driven by an internal urgency.

Combined presentation

When both symptom types are present, we document their impact on work, school, and relationships.

Co-occurring mood or anxiety

Many clients have overlapping symptoms. We clarify diagnosis to guide treatment priorities.

Teen and young adult context

We consider academic load, sleep patterns, procrastination cycles, and digital distractions.

Safety and follow-up needs

Your plan includes check-ins, care coordination, and practical tools for daily function.

After evaluation, support continues

You may receive medication management, psychotherapy, executive functioning support, and practical routines that improve focus over time. We adjust treatment as your life demands change.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide ADHD testing for adults and teens?

Yes. We assess adolescents and adults using age-appropriate evaluation tools and interviews.

Can ADHD visits be done virtually?

Yes. Most evaluation and follow-up care can be completed through secure telehealth sessions.

What happens after testing?

We discuss findings, possible diagnoses, and treatment recommendations that fit your personal goals and schedule.

Can I receive medication management if needed?

Yes, when clinically appropriate, medication support can be part of your ongoing care plan.

Ready for clarity and a clear next step?

Tell us what you are experiencing and we will help you move forward with confidence.
