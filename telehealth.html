Virtual care

Your appointment can happen anywhere

HIPAA-compliant, fully private telehealth sessions deliver the same quality of care as in-person visits, from wherever you are in Pennsylvania or Maryland.

How it works

Real care, real provider, just on your screen

Telehealth at The Place for Recovery is a live, private one-on-one session with the same provider. You get the same depth of conversation, treatment planning, and follow-up without the commute.

Whether you are managing an existing condition or starting fresh, telehealth removes logistical barriers without sacrificing quality.

  • No commute, no waiting room, no parking
  • All you need is a phone, tablet, or computer
  • Fully encrypted and HIPAA-compliant platform
  • Every in-person service is available virtually
Everything, virtually

What is available via telehealth

All of our core services can be delivered virtually. Here is what you can access from home.

Psychiatric evaluations

Full diagnostic assessments with the same depth and clinical rigor as in-person visits.

Medication management

Prescription reviews, adjustments, and monitoring handled remotely with full documentation.

Individual therapy

Private one-on-one therapy sessions including CBT, DBT, ACT, and other evidence-based approaches.

Substance use support

Medication management for substance use and counseling follow-ups without the need to travel.

Family sessions

Multiple family members can join from different locations, making support more accessible.

Follow-up appointments

Routine progress reviews, prescription renewals, and care plan updates from your couch.

Getting started

Your first telehealth visit, step by step

Book your visit

Use our booking link or call to choose a telehealth appointment time that fits your schedule.

Complete your intake forms

Secure digital paperwork takes about 15 minutes and helps us use your appointment time well.

Find a private space

Choose a quiet place where you feel comfortable talking. Headphones can help with privacy.

Join your session

Click the link in your confirmation email. No downloads required, and the session is encrypted.

Where we serve

Pennsylvania and Maryland

If you are located in Pennsylvania or Maryland and have a stable internet connection, we can see you. Telehealth works on phone, tablet, or computer.

  • Pennsylvania residents across all regions
  • Maryland residents across all regions
  • Evening and weekend options when available
