Insurances and Payments

Clear payment options and transparent coverage guidance

We work to make care accessible. Coverage details can vary by plan and state, so our team helps you verify benefits before treatment starts.

Insurance

We accept many major insurance plans and can verify psychiatric, therapy, and telehealth benefits before your first appointment.

Self-pay options

If you are out-of-network, we offer private-pay options and practical visit planning to keep care consistent.

HSA/FSA support

Many clients use HSA or FSA funds for eligible behavioral health services. We can provide receipts for documentation.

Coverage for onsite wellness and lab services

Some services may be billed separately or have different coverage requirements. Our team will guide you through verification before you schedule.

Lab testing & DNA Med Match

General lab testing, drug testing, neurotransmitter panels, and genetic medication matching may have plan-specific coverage or require separate billing.

Primary care & weight management

Routine visits, preventive care, chronic disease management, and weight loss programs are verified based on your plan benefits and eligibility.

What to have ready for verification

  • Insurance card (front and back)
  • Date of birth and contact information
  • Current medication list
  • Preferred pharmacy and primary concerns

Need help understanding your options?

Our team can explain what is covered, expected costs, and recommended visit types so there are no surprises.
