Our Services

Comprehensive mental health services under one roof

Our integrated care model combines psychiatric diagnosis, medication management, psychotherapy, and substance use support. Services are available in-office and through secure telehealth visits.

What we offer

Organized care with clear next steps

We bring evaluation, medication, therapy, and recovery support together so your care stays consistent and collaborative from day one.

Psychiatric Evaluation

Comprehensive assessments that clarify diagnosis and guide a practical treatment plan.

Medication Management

Thoughtful prescribing, close monitoring, and clear education at every visit.

Psychotherapy Services

Evidence-based therapy options for individuals, families, and groups across age ranges.

Substance Use Treatment

Medication-assisted support and counseling for opioid and alcohol use disorder recovery.

Diagnosis & Treatment

Psychiatric diagnosis and treatment planning

A clear diagnosis is where every good treatment plan starts. We take time to do a thorough evaluation so your care is informed, accurate, and built around your real life.

What we diagnose and treat

Major Depressive Disorder Generalized Anxiety Panic Disorder Bipolar Disorder Schizophrenia OCD PTSD ADHD Autism Spectrum Disorders Eating Disorders Sleep Disorders Personality Disorders

What to expect

  • A full psychiatric intake that is never rushed
  • Diagnosis explained clearly in plain language
  • Treatment options reviewed together so your input matters
  • Follow-up built into every plan from day one
Medication management

Thoughtful prescribing with ongoing monitoring

Medication is not one-size-fits-all. We select options based on your history, goals, and response, then monitor closely and adjust as needed.

Antidepressants

SSRIs, SNRIs, and other agents for depression, anxiety, OCD, PTSD, and related conditions.

Mood Stabilizers

For bipolar disorder and mood dysregulation, with routine monitoring when needed.

Antipsychotics

Oral and long-acting injectable options for schizophrenia and severe mood disorders.

ADHD Medications

Stimulant and non-stimulant options for children and adults, tailored to your routine.

Anti-Anxiety Options

Responsible, targeted prescribing with long-term stability in mind.

Long-Acting Injectables

Monthly injectables for patients who prefer not to manage daily oral medication.

  • Clear explanation of benefits and side effects
  • Regular follow-ups and dose adjustments
  • Lab monitoring when clinically needed
  • Telehealth check-ins for convenience
Substance use recovery

Medication-assisted support without judgment

Substance use disorders are medical conditions, not moral failures. We provide Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT) alongside counseling and relapse-prevention planning.

Opioid use disorder

Buprenorphine (Suboxone / Subutex)

Reduces cravings and withdrawal while stabilizing daily life.

Naltrexone (Vivitrol)

Monthly injectable option for patients committed to abstinence-based recovery.

Methadone referrals

We coordinate referrals to appropriate treatment centers when needed.

Alcohol use disorder

Naltrexone (oral / injectable)

Reduces cravings and the rewarding effects of drinking.

Acamprosate

Supports abstinence after detox by restoring chemical balance.

Disulfiram (Antabuse)

A deterrent medication for patients who want strong behavioral support.

Psychotherapy

Evidence-based therapy options

Therapy plans are personalized to goals, developmental stage, and clinical needs.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Identify and reframe thought patterns driving anxiety, depression, and unhelpful behaviors.

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)

Mindfulness, emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and interpersonal effectiveness.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)

Commit to values-driven action while learning to accept difficult thoughts and feelings.

Interpersonal Therapy (IPT)

Build healthier relationships and communication, especially during life transitions.

Individual Therapy

One-on-one sessions in a confidential space with support tailored to your goals.

Family & Group Therapy

Strengthen support systems, improve communication, and rebuild trust together.

Play Therapy (Children)

Age-appropriate support that helps children express emotions and build coping skills.

Whole-person wellness

Weight management, primary care, and onsite lab services

We pair psychiatric care with wellness and medical services that support long-term health, recovery, and daily function.

Weight Loss Management

Postpartum Weight Management

Common questions

Things people usually ask

Clear answers to help you decide on the next step.

Do I need a referral to make an appointment?

No referral is required. You can book directly, and we will verify coverage if your plan requires it.

Do you offer both therapy and medication management?

Yes. You can choose either or both, and we coordinate everything under one plan.

Can I be seen via telehealth if I am in Maryland?

Yes. We are licensed in Pennsylvania and Maryland, so telehealth visits are available in both states.

Do you see children and teenagers?

Yes. We provide psychiatric care for children, adolescents, and adults, with family involvement when needed.

How long does treatment usually take?

It depends on your goals and needs. Some people improve in a few months, while others benefit from longer-term support.

Is my information kept confidential?

Absolutely. We are HIPAA-compliant for both in-person and telehealth visits.

Comprehensive telehealth services

We understand busy schedules. Our HIPAA-compliant telehealth visits provide many of the same services as in-office appointments while reducing travel time and barriers to care.
