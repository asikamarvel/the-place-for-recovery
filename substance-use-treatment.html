    Substance Use Treatment: Effective and Compassionate Care

    Our substance use treatment programs are designed to address the complexities of addiction and support long-term recovery.

    • Individual counseling to explore underlying issues and build coping strategies
    • Group therapy that fosters connection and shared understanding
    • Long-term injections such as extended-release naltrexone or buprenorphine to reduce cravings and withdrawal

    Why our treatments are effective

    Substance use disorders are multifaceted conditions that require comprehensive solutions. By combining medications with therapy and support, we address both the psychological and physiological aspects of addiction and help individuals improve their overall quality of life.

    Medication management for substance use without judgment

    Substance use disorders are medical conditions, not moral failures. We provide medication management for substance use alongside counseling and relapse-prevention planning.

    Opioid use disorder

    Buprenorphine (Suboxone / Subutex)

    Reduces cravings and withdrawal while stabilizing daily life.

    Naltrexone (Vivitrol)

    Monthly injectable option for patients committed to abstinence-based recovery.

    Methadone referrals

    We coordinate referrals to appropriate treatment centers when needed.

    Alcohol use disorder

    Naltrexone (oral / injectable)

    Reduces cravings and the rewarding effects of drinking.

    Acamprosate

    Supports abstinence after detox by restoring chemical balance.

    Disulfiram (Antabuse)

    A deterrent medication for patients who want strong behavioral support.

    Co-occurring concerns we also address

    Recovery often includes support for related mental health conditions.

    Panic Disorder

    Panic disorder involves recurrent and unexpected panic attacks that create intense fear and discomfort.

    • Rapid heart rate or palpitations
    • Shortness of breath or feeling of choking
    • Dizziness or feeling faint
    • Fear of losing control or dying

    Eating Disorders

    Eating disorders involve unhealthy eating behaviors and distorted body image that impact physical and emotional health.

    • Preoccupation with weight, food, and body shape
    • Severe restriction, binge eating, or purging
    • Emotional distress related to eating habits
    • Changes in energy, mood, or daily function

    Sleep Disorders

    Sleep disorders include difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or disrupted sleep patterns.

    • Insomnia or excessive daytime sleepiness
    • Loud snoring or gasping for air during sleep
    • Unusual behaviors during sleep
    • Difficulty concentrating or low energy
